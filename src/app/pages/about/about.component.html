Irena Tolevska Founder, YATO
The Person Behind the Journeys

Irena Tolevska

Founder · Boutique DMC · North Macedonia & The Balkans

With more than 15 years of experience in the travel industry, Irena's journeys don't begin with a list of places to go. They begin with understanding the people who are travelling.

YATO combines that experience with local knowledge, carefully selected partners and personal involvement in the design and delivery of each journey.

Based in Ohrid, North Macedonia — at the heart of the region she loves and knows deeply — Irena works with companies, travel advisors and individuals who want more than a programme. They want a real experience, thoughtfully delivered.

"I believe travel has the power of changing people. I have seen it from different perspectives — as a traveller, as a guide, as an organiser. It always starts with people."
